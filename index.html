Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Modern Craft

"We specialize in handcrafted wooden boxes — combining traditional techniques with modern design to create pieces that endure."

Founded in 2012, AM Wooden Works began as a small artisan workshop with a single vision: to create wooden boxes that are as beautiful as they are functional. Over a decade later, we have grown into a trusted name across India — serving retailers, corporates, exporters, and individuals who demand nothing but the finest.

Every piece we craft passes through the hands of skilled artisans who have spent years mastering the nuances of wood — its grain, its character, its soul. We don't just make boxes; we create heirlooms.

Our Mission

To deliver handcrafted wooden products that combine precision, beauty, and purpose — accessible to everyone, uncompromising in quality.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted wooden box manufacturer — where every workshop order carries our name and our commitment to excellence.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We work with a wide range of premium hardwoods including teak, sheesham (Indian rosewood), mango wood, walnut, oak, and more. Each wood type is selected for its unique grain, durability, and finish quality. For export orders, we also work with engineered wood options.

Absolutely. We offer laser engraving, hot foil stamping, and hand-painted branding on all our boxes. Simply share your artwork and dimensions, and we'll create a box that perfectly represents your brand. Samples can be arranged before bulk production.

For standard products, there's no minimum order — we cater to single pieces as well. For bulk or custom orders with special designs, the minimum order quantity typically starts at 50 units, though this is negotiable based on design complexity.

Standard products are typically dispatched within 5-7 business days. Custom orders take 10-21 days depending on complexity and quantity. We ship across all of India and internationally. Rush delivery options are available for urgent requirements.

Yes, we strongly encourage sample approval before bulk production. A sample fee applies (refunded on bulk order confirmation). This ensures you see exactly what you're getting — material quality, finish, dimensions, and branding — before committing to a large order.
